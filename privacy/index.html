Privacy Policy for e-Cat-Helper-Publish

Application Name: e-Cat-Helper-Publish (also publicly known as eCatering Helper)
Developer / Data Controller: Ramble Networks
Application Homepage: https://foods.ramblenetworks.in
Effective Date: September 25, 2026

1. Overview & Identity of Data Controller

This Privacy Policy describes how Ramble Networks ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and safeguards information through the web application e-Cat-Helper-Publish (publicly accessible as eCatering Helper at https://foods.ramblenetworks.in and https://ramblenetworks.in).

e-Cat-Helper-Publish is an operational management software built specifically for authorized IRCTC railway catering vendors, station restaurant partners, kitchen staff, and railway platform delivery executives to organize passenger food deliveries on trains across India.

2. Google User Data, OAuth 2.0 & Gmail API Access Disclosure

To automate order dispatch directly from IRCTC transactional emails, e-Cat-Helper-Publish requests authorization to connect to the restaurant partner's Gmail account via Google OAuth 2.0.

Specifically Requested Google Scopes:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.readonly
    Read-only access to automated transactional messages sent by official IRCTC eCatering notification addresses (such as ecatering@irctc.co.in).
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/userinfo.email
    Used strictly to identify the authenticated restaurant account owner's email address.

Strict Operating Rules Regarding Google User Data:

  • Automated Order Emails Only: The application's automated background fetcher scans only messages matching IRCTC eCatering transactional templates (identifying Order ID, Station Code, Train Number, Coach, Berth, PNR, Customer Name, and Meal items).
  • No Personal Correspondence Access: All personal, non-IRCTC, social, or private emails are automatically ignored, filtered out, and never stored, read, or processed.
  • No Human Viewing: No employee, engineer, or contractor at Ramble Networks has access to view raw user email inboxes or message contents. All operations are strictly executed by automated parsing algorithms.
  • No Advertising, Profiling, or Marketing: Google user data and email contents are NEVER used for advertising, behavioral targeting, market profiling, or commercial remarketing.
  • No Sale or Third-Party Transfer: We do not sell, rent, license, or transfer Google user data to third-party data brokers, advertisers, or aggregators.
  • No AI/ML Model Training: Google user data is NEVER used to train generalized artificial intelligence or machine learning models.

Google API Services User Data Policy — Limited Use Disclosure

e-Cat-Helper-Publish's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

3. Non-Google Information Collected

To provide restaurant management features, the platform stores:

  • Restaurant Partner Profile: Restaurant name, outlet ID, station code (e.g. NJP, NDLS), business owner contact name, mobile telephone number, and registered address.
  • Order & Delivery Dispatch Records: Train numbers, train names, coach/berth details, delivery timestamps, payment mode (Cash on Delivery or Prepaid), and fulfillment status (Cooked, In Delivery, Delivered).
  • Authentication Tokens: OAuth tokens stored in secure, encrypted Google Firebase Realtime Database nodes restricted strictly to the authorized tenant.

4. Data Storage, Security & Encryption

All communications between the browser, our servers, and Google APIs are secured with 256-bit SSL/TLS encryption. All data is persisted in isolated, role-restricted Google Firebase database infrastructure. Tokens are never exposed to unauthorized third parties or public endpoints.

5. User Control, Revocation & Account Deletion Policy

Users have complete, continuous control over their connected Google accounts:

  • Instant Revocation: You can disconnect your Google Account at any time inside the app under App Settings → Disconnect, or directly through Google's Account Security dashboard at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Complete Data Deletion: You may request complete, permanent erasure of your restaurant profile, all stored orders, and authorization credentials at any time by contacting care@ramblenetworks.in or ramblenetworks@gmail.com. We process all data deletion requests within 48 business hours.
